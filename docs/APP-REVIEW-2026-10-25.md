# Full app review before 1.0 — 25 October 2026

Reviewed checkout: `569323d` (release 0.9.0). This is a fresh review of the whole game ahead of a
1.0.0 release. It does not repeat the findings of the [20 October review](APP-REVIEW-2026-10-20.md)
(R1–R17), which are all resolved. No application code was changed. The implementation plan
built from these findings is [RELEASE-1.0-PLAN.md](RELEASE-1.0-PLAN.md).

## How the review was done

Eight reviewers each covered one dimension. Each read the code, ran probes on national-format
worlds, and reported findings with file and line references. Every headline claim below was then
checked again against the code, or reproduced, before it went into this document.

| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

## Overall assessment

0.9.0 is a broad, honest and stable game. The engine is pure and deterministic, the saves migrate
from every earlier release (real 0.3.0 and 0.8.0 saves, with matches in progress, load and play
on), the national pyramids are correct in all six countries over 20+ seasons, and every automated
gate passes. Few games in the genre have this many connected systems at 1.0.

What 1.0 needs is different from the last cycle. The October reviews were about systems that
computed the wrong thing. This one is about **how the game feels to play for 60 hours**:

1. **The match does not yet feel great.** Choices move the result by about a tenth of a goal per
   match, several situations have one dominant answer, ratings swing far more than real ones, and
   the pitch plays each minute in under a second and then sits still. A goal passes in 1.6 s.
2. **The career goes hollow in its prime.** Fame, fans, cosmetics and money all saturate by about
   24–25; strikers win nearly every individual award; and a loyal striker can top the all-time
   Hall of Fame without ever playing in the top flight.
3. **Pacing asks too much.** A tier-six season is 46 matches with no per-match simulate, so the
   choice is to play 300–600 key moments a season or give up the whole season. The first session
   takes 4–7 minutes and about 25 interactions to reach the first key moment.
4. **The world drifts over a legacy.** Elite players inflate (85+ rated: 85 → 215 over 25
   seasons), league tables are far too flat, names repeat inside squads, and club cash grows
   tenfold with nothing to spend it on.
5. **Long saves are slow and will eventually stop saving.** A Continue on a late save takes 4.8 s
   on a fast desktop, mostly copying, re-validating and rewriting the whole 75 MB world. A world's
   history is capped at 100 entries by validation but never trimmed, so season 101 cannot be saved.

There are also two P0 defects, both small to fix, and a handful of release-hygiene gaps (no
feedback channel, no privacy page, real club names in the shipped code, Safari never tested on a
device).

## What is working well

1. **Architecture.** The engine is pure and lint-enforced: no `Math.random`, no browser globals,
   2 `any` in 97k lines, `strict` and `noUncheckedIndexedAccess` on. Seeded determinism holds.
2. **Save safety on the main paths.** One transaction per write, revision checks, Web Locks per
   tab, prototype-safe bounded imports. Old saves load: the reviewer built real 0.3.0 and 0.8.0
   saves (including in-progress match-16 and match-20 sessions), loaded them in 0.9.0, finished
   the matches and ran 70 more weeks with validation passing.
3. **Correct pyramids.** Season-one movements match [REALISM.md](REALISM.md) exactly in all six
   countries, league sizes hold for 20 seasons, and 45 season-end snapshots had zero invariant
   violations (squad sizes, registration, numbers, contracts, reserve rules).
4. **Honest odds.** The 10,000-match distribution is realistic, stated odds equal realised odds,
   and every factor is explained.
5. **Design system.** Both themes, the green and gold identity, the desktop decision layout, the
   shortcuts dialog, the tutorial and the empty states are release quality.
6. **The career wrapper.** Stories, lasting facts, preseason, the rival, the national team, the
   Chronicle, Moments and child careers give the game an identity no competitor has in a free
   browser game.
