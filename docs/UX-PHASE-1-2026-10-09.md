# UX improvements: Phase 1

Completed on 9 October 2026 on branch `ux/phase-1`. Covers findings 1, 4 and 9 in the [end-user review](UX-REVIEW-2026-10-09.md). Existing gameplay and balance changes in this workspace were preserved.

## Player-visible changes

Training edits survive navigation between career pages without changing the committed training plan. A separate pending-training notice links back to the draft. **Save training** applies it explicitly; **Discard changes** restores the committed plan. Phones keep these actions above the bottom navigation and bring focused controls into view, including when text size changes.

Advancing a week, continuing to matchday, simulating a season or starting the next season with pending training presents **Save and continue**, **Continue with saved training**, and **Keep editing**. Browser Back cancels the requested operation. Saving writes the proposed training before applying it to the active world; a storage failure preserves the old committed plan and the draft and does not start simulation. Slot ownership and career context are rechecked before application.

Loading, importing, replacing or deleting the active save warns before abandoning pending training. Cancelling retains the draft and does not show a success notice. Nested save confirmations retain working Back behavior. Reloading a page with pending training requests the browser's leave confirmation. Drafts are temporary and excluded from autosaves and exported backups.

Tablet sidebar labels and badges fit without clipping. Page tabs also adapt to the available width at enlarged text sizes. Navigation uses **Gallery** and **Saved games**; slot actions distinguish **Continue career**, **Load world**, and **Load gallery**. Replacement labels describe the current content being written, and deletion uses **Delete save**.

## Verification

- 38 focused unit tests passed across training drafts, session state, autosaving, saves and the worker client. These include failed storage writes, lost locks, stale contexts and all four time-advance types.
- All 30 affected browser cases passed in each of Chromium, Firefox and WebKit: training drafts, save actions, coaching, navigation and foundation flows. WebKit's phone layout was repeated three times after making its assertions wait for text-resize observers to settle.
- Final tablet/page-tab navigation checks passed across all three browsers. At 768 × 1024, ordinary and 130% text and `99+` badges retain sidebar and document widths without horizontal overflow.
- Typecheck, lint, production build and route bundle audit passed. The menu loads 106.3 KB gzipped JavaScript; all 27 audited routes remain below the 300 KB budget.
- Compared the original review screenshots with the finished phone and tablet layouts and inspected the training gate and save screen. Browser regressions check console and page errors.

Firefox requires running these checks outside this Windows filesystem sandbox: its tab subprocess fails before loading even an empty page inside the sandbox. The same installed browser passed the affected journeys outside it. Production builds used Vite's `--configLoader runner` to avoid the sandbox's configuration-bundling restriction.
