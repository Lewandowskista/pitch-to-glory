# Playtests

The review's recall protocol ([APP-REVIEW-2026-10-20.md](APP-REVIEW-2026-10-20.md), "Balance and
playtest plan"), to run with people after the story and career changes (review plan phase 6,
step 6.6). Automated profiles measure numbers; only players can say whether a career tells a
story.

## Setup

- A new career on the current release, played for at least a season and a half (into a
  preseason), and one late-career save (`artifacts/performance/late.json`) for a veteran's view.
- Players new to the game and players who have played before, separately.
- No coaching during play; note where people get stuck, but do not help unless they ask.

## During play

Every few in-game weeks, ask the player to explain why they chose what they just chose: a
key-moment choice, a commitment, a press answer, a story choice, a contract decision.

## After play: recall

Without the log, the inbox or the Chronicle in view, ask them to recount the last few weeks
and the season so far: what happened, to whom, and why it mattered.

| They remember                                                                                         | What it says                                       |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Only a rating, a level and a new club                                                                 | The systems need stronger consequences and people  |
| A risky return, a kept promise, a rival meeting, a decisive save, a manager who stopped trusting them | The breadth is producing the intended career story |

Then ask:

1. What did the manager expect of you this season, and did you meet it?
2. Which choice cost you something you noticed later?
3. Who in the game do you remember by name, and why?
4. Was there a moment you stopped reading and just pressed Continue? Where?
