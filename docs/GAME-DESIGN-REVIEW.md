# Game design, mechanics and balancing review — 8 October 2026

**Scope.** Every simulation system as shipped on `main` (6911666): progression and ageing, the match engine and key moments, the economy and market, social and honours systems, the simulated world and team selection. The question was not "does it work" (it does; see [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md)) but "does it play as the design in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) intends", and where not, what to change.

**Method.**

- Five independent code reviews, one per system, working from the constants in `src/engine/config.ts` and the formulas in `src/engine/`, with the arithmetic shown. Several claims were re-verified in code for this document; the rest are traced to file and line.
- Four complete careers played headlessly from 17 to retirement at 34 with the soak policy (`scripts/soak-career.ts`), one per position, with the numbers a balancing review needs recorded every season: ability and its percentile in the world, squad rank, tier, role, wage, cash, fame, appearances, goals, assists, ratings, injuries, trust, awards, caps, offers, the rival, and the world's own ability distribution. Two careers spent attribute points on the position's key attributes; one spread them evenly. Results are in [Appendix A](#appendix-a-four-careers-to-retirement).
- A twelve-season national world simulated without a career, measuring ability drift, champions, goals, appearances and sackings.
- A three-season trace of trust, fan affection, morale, form and chemistry week by week.

## Assessment

The foundations are strong: the engine is deterministic, the stated probabilities of key moments are honest (over 11,800 decisions across four careers the stated and actual success rates agree within one point in every bucket), goals per game and home advantage are realistic, ability does not drift over a dozen seasons, and the breadth of systems is unusual. What is missing is **pressure**: almost nothing in the game pushes back, so a career is a sequence of pleasant screens rather than a struggle.

Five structural problems explain nearly every symptom:

1. **Two ability scales that do not agree.** Matches resolve on the one or two attributes a choice uses, while selection, market value, the national team, awards and the Hall of Fame all use the unweighted mean of 22 attributes. The career's point economy (~640 points over 17 seasons) cannot lift that mean past the mid-40s when the top tier sits at 67–78, so by the engine's own measure the player is a lower-league squad filler for life, even while scoring 250 goals. Every career in Appendix A retired at peak ability 33–45, last or near-last in their own squad, with no cap, while the rival reached 73–79 and tier 1 by 21 without playing a minute.
2. **Decisions that are not decisions.** Every choice in a key moment has the same expected goals at the reference level, the quality hint prints the answer, attacking mentality is a pure buff, skills do not stack, momentum changes nothing, substitutions never happen and the half-time talk's only effect is never applied. The player's best policy is "pick the choice that uses your highest attribute, always attack", which is also what the UI tells them.
3. **Relationships that only go up.** Trust and fan affection have no decay, reach 100 inside two seasons and stay there; morale settles at a fixed point; chemistry, cliques, culture fit and the rival's intensity feed nothing mechanical. The press has a dominant answer. The manager's promise is worth three good matches of trust against a value that is already capped.
4. **Money without stakes.** Wages span 50 to ~1,900 a week (38:1; football is ~600:1), the club you play for barely changes your wage, cash has no sink that matters, negotiation is solved by asking 1.45× every time, offers arrive at ~15 a season, and a club can extend an expiring contract at the same wage forever. The striker in Appendix A was on a "youth" contract at 34.
5. **A world that does not move.** Club reputation is never changed after generation, so no club can rise or fall; AI transfers are 36 random free swaps a season; benches never play (half of all top-tier squad players make zero appearances); background goals and ratings ignore ability, so awards are position lotteries; title races are near coin-flips; only the bottom club ever sacks its manager.

None of this needs new screens. It is constants, a handful of formulas, six or seven real defects, and tests that assert the dynamics rather than the plumbing. The recommended order is in [the plan](#recommended-plan).

## Findings by system

Constants are in `src/engine/config.ts` unless stated. Proposed values are starting points to be confirmed by the tests in Phase E.

### 1. Progression and ageing

- **The attribute economy is half the size the ability metric needs.** XP ≈ 125 a match (72% of it minutes: 90 × 1.2 = 108), ~5,600 a season → level 13 after one season, 30 after four, 52 after seventeen (`levelXpBase` 300 × 1.06ⁿ). 52 levels × 8 points = 416, plus ~200 from training ≈ 640. Reaching the soft cap on all 22 attributes needs ~1,320. Keepers need ~413 for their seven and reach tier-1 ability by 22. Meanwhile an AI player of potential 81 is at 73 on every attribute at 21 (`growthPerSeason` 0.75). Measured: ability 20 → 44 over a career (Appendix A); a fully specialised build reads as 55.
  Change: (a) position-weighted `playerAbility` (the position's emphasised attributes × 3, category weights by position) used everywhere the mean is used now; (b) passive growth for the career player toward cap at ~35% of the gap a season for unfocused attributes, so points buy _emphasis_ rather than _existence_; (c) `levelXpBase` 500, `levelXpGrowth` 1.02 and `attributePointsPerLevel` 5 so levels keep coming at 25+ (13/7/4.5/3 a season at 17/21/26/33) and 99 is reachable.
- **XP ignores performance.** A 5.0 rating earns 108, an 8.0 with a goal 188. Change `match.xp` to {perMinute 0.5, ratingThreshold 5.5, perRating 50, perGoal 40, perAssist 28}, scale by absolute opposition reputation `(0.7 + 0.006 × rep)`, and credit key decisions (+4 per success, +8 when the odds were below 0.5). International caps: `xp.cap` 25 → 120.
- **Soft caps can be bypassed at any age.** Beyond cap + 5 costs a flat 3; a 17-year-old can hold finishing 99 (73 points) in season one and keep it to 30. Appendix A's striker did exactly this. Change: `beyondCap = 3 + floor((value − cap − 5)/4)` and a hard stop at cap + 12 before 24.
- **Training has a dominant plan.** Gain per unit of injury risk: low 40, normal 26, high 14.6; three high sessions ≈ 19 weeks injured a season for +9 points. The mentor session beats a normal session on every axis. Change: high {gain 0.24, risk 0.009, fatigue 4}, low {gain 0.09, fatigue 0.5}, injury risk × 0.7 under 21 and × 1.3 over 30, `weeklyRecovery` 14 and session fatigue {0.5, 1.5, 4, extra 2, recovery −8} (fixes D12), mentor base 0.12 × 1.0–1.5.
- **Skill points saturate.** The outfield tree costs 77 points, keepers 36; the player earns ~53. Everything relevant is bought by level ~45; afterwards points are dead currency. Change: tier costs 2/3/4/5 and minimum levels 1/8/16/25; stacking per D6 (`1.2 × 1.1^(n−1)`, cap 1.45) or a unique effect per capstone.
- **Ageing is right; forced retirement is not.** Pace from 26, physical 28, technical 30, mental 34: as specified. Forced at 40 only, when a potential-88 player is still ~63. Change: force at 38, or when ability < 0.78 × peak after 33; show in the attribute UI that points above cap decay from the category's peak age.
- **Career-threatening injuries are not.** 1.2% of injuries × 25% ≈ 7% of careers see one; it costs −6 pace and −6 acceleration. Change: −12 pace/acceleration, −6 agility/jumping, 30% chance of a second ligament injury within two seasons.
- **Archetypes are cosmetic**: a 24–29 point spread (`emphasisScale` 1) against ~100 level points in season one. Change: `emphasisScale` 2.5 and one systemic perk each.
- **Positions are learned only in training**: familiarity never grows from minutes played out of position and carries no match penalty. Change: +3 per 90 minutes in the slot; attribute penalty `(100 − familiarity) × 0.1` in that slot.

### 2. The match engine and key moments

- **Choices are EV-anchored, so there is nothing to decide.** At the reference level every choice in a situation has identical expected goals (ST box chance: all 0.1307). The UI shows the probability, the goal chance on success and the attributes used, so the best choice is the one using your highest attribute, every time. Change: give choices distinct risk profiles rather than equal EV — e.g. `far-post` `forOnSuccess` 0.85 (rebounds exist; today `failureGoal` is always 0 because every choice keeps `forOnSuccess: 1`), `near-post` odds × 0.95; scale pass outcomes by the receiver's finishing `(1 + (finishing − level) × 0.01)` so passing to a good striker is a real option; show a five-band quality label by default and the exact probability only in the breakdown.
- **Attacking mentality and attacking roles are strictly dominant.** `rates()` keeps the total fixed and shifts share ± 0.03, so own expected goals rise _and_ the opponent's fall by the same amount (balanced [1.454, 1.246] → attacking [1.535, 1.165]); `cut-inside`/`push-forward` likewise; `track-back` is a pure nerf. The captain's "push" is always right. Change: scale totals asymmetrically — attacking own × 1.08 / opponent × 1.06, defensive own × 0.92 / opponent × 0.90 — and give `track-back` its share back so its identity is the counter relief alone. Risk: `high` shots odds × 0.95 with rebound 0.9, `low` × 1.03.
- **One striker owns 55% of the team's goals.** `shares.ST` 0.55 (`config.ts:32`), the selected player is excluded from background goals, and goals out-reward assists (XP 30 vs 20, fame 2 vs 0, headlines, bonuses). Probe: 0.80 goals a match (~30 a season at any level); Appendix A: 252 goals, 11 assists. Change: shares ST 0.40, wingers 0.32, AM 0.32; `perAssist` 28; fame +1 per assist.
- **The player is ~30× more influential than any teammate, and squad quality barely matters.** Ten teammates +10 ability → +0.044 goals; the player +20 → +19% team xG; a 90 in a 60 league ≈ +27%. Change: `squadWeight` 0.4 → 0.6 / `reputationWeight` 0.6 → 0.4 in `strength.ts` (with D7's reputation dynamics), caps `attributeMaximum` 1.6 → 1.45 and `impactMaximum` 1.3 → 1.2.
- **Momentum, possession, half-time and substitutions are cosmetic.** Momentum feeds no probability; substitutions need fatigue 82 and a player starting at ~60 to reach it (0 in 1,200 probe matches, 0 in 4 careers); half-time talk effects are never applied (D2). Change: odds factor `1 + (momentum − 50) × 0.002` shown as a step; `fatiguePerMinute` 0.8, `substitutionFatigue` 70, plus a tactical sub roll at 60–80′ (p ≈ 0.2 when rating < 6 or leading by 2); `motivate` momentum +8 and × 1.03 second half; `complain` × 0.97 unless the request is granted; apply the trust delta.
- **Rating is capped for keepers and ignores the result.** Keepers' max ≈ 8.0 in 300 matches; a centre-back in a 0–5 with no failed moment rates 6.6; `≥ 9` for ST 4%, CB 0.3%. Change: `stop` 1.5 → 2.2 for direct saves; result term ± 0.25; clean sheet +0.4 for GK and defenders.
- **Fame excludes non-scorers.** `round(max(0, r − 6.5) × 2 + 2 × goals)`: CB/GK ≈ 1 a match vs ST 3; defenders reach fame level 8 in 11 seasons, 10 never (Appendix A: 454 vs 1,075). Change: `ratingThreshold` 6.3, `perRating` 3, +1 per assist, +1.5 per clean sheet, `perGoal` 1.5; decay 10% of fame above 100 at each rollover so fame is a standing, not a lifetime counter.
- **Objectives: two fixed kinds, one unachievable.** The passing objective (≥ 85%) completes 7–8% for non-strikers (routine pass p = 0.68 + passing × 0.002). Change: target = `clamp(round((0.68 + passing × 0.002) × 100) + 4, 70, 92)`; rotate kinds seeded per fixture (score, assist, 3 tackles, 3 saves, 2 shots, no error).
- **Trait value is skewed to low-probability choices** (× 1.2 on 13% is +17% goals; on 86% it is +2.4%). For base ≥ 0.75 apply the trait to impact (× 1.1) instead. "One-on-one" is 91% saved (real ≈ 35–45%): `defenceWeight` 1.4 → 4 with position weight 0.7. Five skills are never read in a match (`professional`, `second-wind`, `iron-man`, `leader`, `captains-voice`) and `big-game-player` never fires in league games (importance 1).
- **The statistical gate does not guard the player's experience.** Tested: goals, home > away, draws, reputation upsets. Not tested: score tails, ability-gap upsets, career-player goals by position (would have caught 0.8), rating distribution by position, moment counts, objective completion, XP/fame bands, substitution frequency, best-vs-worst-policy spread. See Phase E.

### 3. The economy and market

- **Wages are compressed and club stature barely matters.** `ability² × (0.12 + rep/1000) × role`: tier-6 ≈ 50, tier-1 typical 1,180, star 1,900 (38:1; real ≈ 600:1); a star at tier 3 earns 82% of a top-club wage. Appendix A: 50 → 271 over a career at tiers 2–4. Change: `wage = max(50, ability² × (0.05 + 0.45 × (rep/100)²)) × role` → tier-1 typical 1,680, star 3,430, star at tier 3 42% of that; check wage budgets (25 × 1,680 < 8 × 78²).
- **Cash is meaningless after three seasons.** Sponsor fees grow × 1.6 per fame level and reach ~7× the wage at level 10; the only sinks are eight lifestyle items whose morale effect is ≤ +1.6% key-moment odds, and the overspend check compares upkeep to wage only (a star is penalised −3 for a villa, a semi-pro −1 for a flat). The start-up investment is +22% a year with ~3% chance of any loss. Change: price lifestyle in wage-weeks (hatch 25 … villa 400; upkeep = cost/250 a week); compare overspend to wage + sponsors; `feeGrowth` 1.6 → 1.35 and cap sponsor fees at 1.5 × wage; give homes a tangible effect (−2/−3 weekly fatigue); start-up `spread` 0.08 with a 2%/season wipe-out, fund `spread` 0.012.
- **Negotiation is solved; agents are interchangeable.** Ceiling ≥ 1.12 × opening, walk-away only above 1.3 × ceiling, counter = min(ceiling, midpoint): asking 1.45× on every axis is risk-free and lands on the hidden ceiling. Net wage after commission differs by < 2% between no agent, aggressive, connected and economical; late career every agent is net negative because commission also takes sponsor money. Change: hidden walk-away = opening × U(1.2, 1.35); counters move a third of the gap; over-asking costs trust (renewals) or a 20% walk-away on three or more axes; commission on wage, signing-on and loyalty only; aggressive `agentStretch` 0.006, connected foreign reach `(1 + network/60)` with `pitchChance` 0.001, economical stretch 0.001.
- **Offers are spam, and lateral.** Ten tracked clubs, re-bidding every window (D5), with any club of reputation ≥ parent − 5 admitted, so a settled star gets ~15 offers a season, many for a "backup" role. Change: on decline, confidence 25 and a two-window cooldown; `maximumTransfer` 5, `offerChance` 0.25; admit a club only if `deservedRole` there ≥ the current role or reputation ≥ parent + 10, unless a request is in; carry half of a request's trust loss to the new club's fans.
- **Contract roles drive selection, not ability.** A key promise floors starts at 90% whatever the form, fatigue or competition; a backup inside the best XI starts 53%. Change: `keyFloor` 0.75 and only when `placesOutside ≤ 1`; `perPlaceOutside` 0.08–0.10; `inTeam` +0.20; promise thresholds key 0.8 / rotation 0.5; when a promise breaks, halve the release clause and open renewal talks at the deserved role.
- **AI money is cosmetic and the lowest tier goes bust silently.** Income 12 × rep², costs 3 × rep², wage floor 50 × 22–26 players: a rep-10 club loses 200–450 a week, hits zero within two seasons and can never bid; top clubs only accumulate (6.5M, 9.0M). Change: AI wage floor 25 (career player keeps 50) or income floor 4,800; reset transfer budgets each rollover; spend budgets (see 5); at balance 0 release the two highest earners.
- **Sponsorships are always +EV**; failure costs the eight-week bonus and −3 fame. Change: failure −(3 + level) fame, 25% clawback, category locked two seasons; success renews at × 1.25.
- Loans are fine; negotiable bonuses, sell-on and loyalty from the spec are fixed constants.

### 4. Social, narrative and honours

- **Trust and fans are ratchets; morale is a fixed point.** Trust +0.75 a match plus +0.3 a week from seniors plus press ≈ +50 a season, no decay: 55 → 100 within two seasons (trace: 50 → 94 in one). Effects at 100: +0.10 start chance, +10 morale. Morale target ≈ 80 (range 68–95) with a two-week half-life; the match effect is ± 5% at the extremes. Form's steady state is 10 × rating ≈ 67 and it moves nothing (+0.02 selection, +1 moment). Change: weekly decay toward 60 for trust and fans (`value += (60 − value) × 0.03`); `matchMorale.slope` 0.004 with bounds 0.88/1.12 and a non-odds effect (fatigue × (1 + (70 − morale)/200)); `formRatingWeight` 0.25 / retention 0.75 and `formStep` 10.
- **Chemistry, cliques, leaders and culture fit feed nothing.** Outside `social/` they appear only on the Club page and in the retirement screen; their whole effect on key-moment odds is ≤ ± 0.6%. Change: team choices (square pass, cross, through ball) scale by `1 + (chemistry − 60) × 0.004`; dressing-room mood shifts background expected goals ± 0.05; a hostile seniors clique (< 35) takes 0.05 off the start chance; culture fit multiplies training gain by `0.8 + 0.4 × fit/100` and fit < 40 lowers the deserved role one rank — all shown on the Club page.
- **The press has a dominant answer.** Every topic offers a safe +trust/+mood answer and a +3 fame "confident" answer whose cost is trust or clique affinity, both inert; confident is strictly best for fame. Change: answers resolve against the next result (confident then a loss: fame −2, fans −3; humble then a win: trust +3) and coverage feeds scouting confidence.
- **The rival develops for free and is compared unfairly.** Potential = player's ± 2, then AI growth to potential by ~25 regardless of minutes, while the player is XP-gated; the comparison credits background ratings 0.8 a goal vs the career's 0.6; intensity only gates press frequency. Appendix A: rival 73–79 and tier 1 by 21–23 in every run. Change: rival growth × `(0.7 + rating/20)` so a benched rival stalls (needs D8); unify goal credit at 0.6; intensity adds to the head-to-head matchup and a rival's move bumps scouting.
- **Awards have no league weighting.** Young Player and the Golden Ball score `10 × rating + 1.5 × goals + assists` at every tier, with candidates from tier 1 plus the player's league. A tier-6 winger won three player-of-the-month awards, team of the season and Young Player of the Year in his first season; the striker won a tier-3 Golden Boot, MVP and team of the season with 21 goals in 14 games (Appendix A). Change: multiply by the scouting visibility of the tier (1, 0.85, 0.7, 0.55, 0.45, 0.4) and restrict the Golden Ball to tiers ≤ 2; make background stats ability-aware (D9).
- **The Hall of Fame overpays routine awards and ignores AI honours.** Monthly awards score 10 each, the same as league MVP; AI players rank on club stats only. A tier-4 midfielder with 0 goals ranked 4,389th of 60,707; the tier-2/3 striker 159th. Change: weight awards 2/3/10/40 by kind and count AI winners from `world.awards` and `world.trophies`.
- **The national team is unreachable at youth level and needs potential ≥ 84 at senior.** Score = ability + 0.1 × form + min(8, 0.02 × fame) against every player of the nationality, so a 17-year-old at 35–45 competes with academy players at 72. Zero caps in all four careers. Change: rank youth candidates by projected ability (potential × age curve); senior `fameCap` 14, fame weight 0.03, +0.5 per season award held; add a national-team term to scouting visibility.
- **The season goal and the promise are mostly trivial.** 150 passes ≈ 4.5 matches (a key midfielder completes ~1,300); +1 attribute ≈ two weeks; the appearance promise is 98% for a key player and 34% for a backup at the same stakes; +6/−3 trust against a capped value. Change: passes 600, attribute +3 (season +6), appearances only for key/rotation; on achievement grant something trust cannot buy (+1 role rank for the season, or +5 fans); on a miss, −0.05 on the promised role's start base for six weeks.
- Forced retirement is age-only (40); the spec's "forced by decline" is absent. The previous club's fan affection persists but is never read.

### 5. The simulated world and selection

- **Title races are coin-flips.** Tier-1 band 72–84 with `strengthScale` 0.012: a reputation-84 side at home to a mid-table 76 wins 48%, away to a promoted 60 wins 31%. Twelve different Portuguese champions in twelve seasons; champions average ~66 of 114 points; a reputation-82 club finished bottom the year after winning. Change: `strengthScale` 0.024, `reputationTierStep` 12 with ceiling 99 (band 75–87); re-run the upset assertions.
- **No club can rise or fall (D7)** and **there is no AI transfer market**: one random same-position swap per country per window, fee 0 (36 a season for 959 clubs); free agents pile up (2,251 unsigned by season 12). Change: reputation moves ± 4 a season toward the new tier's band and by finishing position, +2 for a continental win; per window, clubs with budget bid market value for the best player of a lower-reputation club who beats their weakest slot by ≥ 3; sellers over `squadTarget` accept; refill from free agents.
- **AI squads never rotate and never get injured (D8)**; **background stats ignore ability (D9)**. Change: AI effective ability × `1 − max(0, fatigue − 40)/150` with a fixture-seeded ± 3 jitter; AI injuries with the career table in `finalizeFixture`; scorer weight × `max(0.2, 1 + (finishing − team mean)/25)`, assists by passing + vision, rating + `(ability − match mean) × 0.02`.
- **Sackings are wrong-shaped**: only the bottom club, at weeks 12 and 24, 80% under 0.9 ppg → 66–74 sackings a season, clubs sacked twice; `manager.ability` and `personality` are never read. Change: weekly hazard `0.04 × max(0, expectedPPG(rank) − ppg)` after week 8 with a 15-week cooldown; manager ability `(ability − 60) × 0.05` into team strength; ambition biases the intake and key promises.
- **Selection is contract-driven (see 3)**: role spans 0.52 of the start probability, competition 0.28, form 0.12; the formation-fit factor is worth +0.08, so learning a position barely changes playing time. A 17-year-old trialist at ability 20 starts 76% of games from day one on a rotation contract.
- **No 90+ players exist** (max potential 0.75 × 84 + 13 + 8 = 84; measured world max 86.7–88.3, 2–7 players ≥ 85 of ~23,000). Change: `talentSpread` 12 with a right-skewed draw above reputation 75, `peakBase` 15.
- **Generation artefacts**: 11% of players aged 35+ at generation (3.9% at equilibrium), 8% foreign in every league (real top tiers 35–65%), every academy player at a big club has potential 68–84, 25% two-footed. Change: triangular age draw peaking at 25; foreign share `0.05 + 0.6 × clamp((rep − 40)/50)`; intake potential `rep × 0.6 + 10 ± 12`; two-footed 5%.
- **Continental cups**: 64 of ~111 top-flight clubs qualify (France 11 of 18); no prize money or reputation. Change: 16-club cups, champions `{3,3,3,3,2,2}`, prize `rep² × 40` per group match, winner +2 reputation.
- Background and interactive matches share one strength model and agree on goals (2.70 a game, home 1.49 / away 1.21, H/D/A 44/25/31); ability does not drift over twelve seasons; squads stay 22–26. Lifecycle weeks are mis-placed (D10).

## Recommended plan

Ordered by how much of the experience each unlocks and by dependency. Every phase ends with a measurable gate run by the career-profile script (Phase E's first deliverable, built from the review instrument) so that balance is asserted, not eyeballed.

**Phase A — one ability scale (progression).** Position-weighted ability; passive growth toward cap; the new level curve and XP weights; soft-cap hardening; skill stacking and tier costs; training and recovery constants (D6, D11, D12); hidden attributes wired in; familiarity from minutes. _Gate:_ a median career reaches position-weighted ability 70+ by 25 and the top tier by 24–26; a keeper and a striker reach it within one season of each other; no attribute above cap + 12 before 24; the balanced and specialised policies finish within 10 ability points.

**Phase B — decisions with trade-offs (match).** Distinct risk profiles per choice and receiver-aware passes; asymmetric mentality and role effects; momentum, fatigue and substitutions that happen; half-time effects applied (D2); goal shares and assist rewards; rating, fame and objective fixes; trait application by probability band. _Gate (500-match profile per position):_ ST 0.35–0.55 goals a match; GK rating p90 ≥ 7.8; passing objective 25–60%; substitutions 5–20%; best-EV vs worst-EV policy spread ≥ 15% in goals; momentum step visible in every breakdown.

**Phase C — a world that moves.** Reputation dynamics (D7), the AI transfer window, rotation and AI injuries (D8), ability-aware background stats (D9), lifecycle weeks (D10), strength scale and tier bands, sackings and manager ability, generation fixes, 90+ players, continental places and prizes. _Gate (12-season world):_ champion median ≥ 70 points; favourite at home ≥ 55%; ≥ 80% of top-tier squad members with ≥ 5 appearances; Spearman(goals, finishing) > 0.3 among strikers; ≥ 3 clubs change tier band in reputation over 12 seasons; sackings 15–30 a season across 52 leagues.

**Phase D — stakes (economy and relationships).** Wage formula and budgets; key-role bids (D1), offer cooldowns (D5), free agency (D3), promise thresholds (D4); negotiation with walk-away; agent differentiation; lifestyle pricing and sponsor risk; trust and fan decay; morale and form responsiveness; chemistry, dressing room and culture fit effects; press resolved against results; rival growth tied to minutes; award weighting; national-team projection; goal and promise targets. _Gate (17-season soak):_ ≤ 60 declined offers; at least one broken promise or transfer request justified by selection; trust spends ≥ 30% of weeks below 70; a tier-6 season wins no national award; a potential-80 career earns ≥ 10 senior caps; wage at tier 1 ≥ 8 × wage at tier 4 for the same ability.

**Phase E — tests that guard dynamics.** Promote the review instrument to `scripts/career-profile.ts` (four positions to retirement, one JSON per run, the tables in Appendix A regenerated); per-position 500-match profile tests; world-dynamics assertions; soak assertions for the gates above; `docs/BALANCING.md` rewritten around the new constants with the measured values beside each. Run the profile in the weekly performance workflow so a regression shows up as a number.

Phases A and B are independent and can run in parallel; C before D because D's selection, award and rival changes depend on reputation dynamics and AI rotation; E accompanies every phase (its gates are written first).

## Appendix B: method notes and caveats

- The soak policy is a conservative player: it never buys lifestyle items, never hires an agent, always answers the press with the first choice, accepts every offer from a higher-reputation club and never declines renewals. Human play would spend more and move less; neither changes the structural findings.
- Two runs pumped a single attribute to 99 on purpose, to test the soft cap; the evenly spread run (`review-d`) reached a similar mean (45 vs 43–44) with a broader profile, confirming that the point supply, not the spending pattern, bounds ability.
- Match-engine probes used generated level-70 worlds and 300–1,200 matches per claim; world measurements used a national world over twelve seasons without a career. Both scripts were temporary and are not in the repository; Phase E recreates the useful parts as kept tools.
- Line references are to `main` at 6911666.
