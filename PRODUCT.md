# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Football fans who want to live one footballer's career without controlling a player in real time or managing a whole club. They play in short sessions on a laptop or a phone browser (often one-handed on a phone, sometimes landscape on desktop), returning to a saved career over weeks. Each session is a loop: check the hub, act on what matters (a fixture, an offer, a recovery decision), play a match through key-moment decisions, read the result and progress.

## Product Purpose

Pitch to Glory is a browser-first football career game: a young player rises from a semi-professional league to a legendary career in a persistent, simulated world, then a new career (or their child's) continues in the same world. Success is a player who always knows their next action, trusts what the game tells them (results, records, awards), understands why a decision succeeded or failed, and wants to come back to see the next chapter of the career story.

## Brand Personality

Bold, sporty, warm. Matchday energy rather than spreadsheet calm: confident display type, the green-and-gold identity, chunky tappable controls, and satisfying moments when something is earned. The voice is a knowledgeable football companion: direct, encouraging, never patronising. Emotional goals: anticipation before a match, tension at a key moment, pride in a reward, attachment to "your player".

References to lean on: console career modes (EA FC Career, NBA 2K MyCareer) for a strong "your player" identity and one prominent next action on the hub; modern sports apps (OneFootball, FotMob) for clear fixture and match hierarchy, live states and comfortable tap targets.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards: identical card grids, tiny uppercase eyebrows over every section, gray admin-panel chrome.
- Dense Football Manager-style screens: walls of tables with no single next action. Depth belongs behind disclosure, not on the surface.
- Long horizontal scrolling tab strips or navigation that jumps position between pages.
- Pay-to-win store patterns or manipulative urgency.

## Design Principles

1. **One obvious next action.** Every screen leads with what the player should do now; secondary detail recedes or sits behind disclosure.
2. **Stable, predictable navigation.** Navigation stays in the same place on every page and every device; moving between pages never shifts the controls under the player's thumb or cursor.
3. **Show the football.** Decisions and results keep their football context (pitch, score, opponent) in view; numbers explain the game rather than replace it.
4. **Earned celebration.** Motion and colour are spent on real moments (a goal, a level-up, a trophy), not on chrome, so rewards feel like rewards.
5. **Teach through transparency.** Every chance, rating and reward can be traced to its causes in plain language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA (Lighthouse accessibility above 95). Full keyboard operability with visible focus; screen-reader labels on every control; font scaling to 130%; reduced-motion mode that removes non-essential movement; colour-blind-safe kit clash detection, and no information carried by colour alone; touch targets at least 44 px; layouts that work at 360 px wide without horizontal scrolling.
